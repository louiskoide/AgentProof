**What your uncommitted changes already had:**
- **Saving:** starred items are kept in localStorage under `td-watchlist-v1`. Bad or blocked storage falls back to an empty, in-memory list.
- **Star buttons:** on player names, on both clubs of each transfer, on story clubs and on story candidates. Placeholders like "Free agent" can't be starred.
- **Filter logic:**
  - A transfer counts as watched if its player, old club or new club is starred.
  - A story counts as watched if its club, any candidate, or any candidate's current club is starred.

**What I added:**
1. **Watchlist toggle** next to the search box, with a count of starred items. It filters both the Transfers and Stories tabs and works alongside search and the league filter.
2. **Wired up the cards:** both card types now receive `watch`, which fixes the crash.
3. **Empty-state messages:** your `emptyMsg` helper was written but never used; now it is. With the filter on and nothing starred, users are told to tap ☆ to star something. With the filter on and no matches, they see "No starred … match your filters." On the Transfers tab this message replaces the three empty columns.
4. **Star hover fix:** in story candidate rows the star's hover background was the same color as the row, so hovering showed nothing. It now uses a different color.

Stars are matched by name, since that's how your changes already stored them. If the live feed ever spells a player or club differently, their star won't carry over.
