I haven't written any code yet. The feature as described depends on data the app doesn't have, so I need you to choose a direction first.

**What's actually in the app:**

- **The campaign dataset has no match results.** Each of the 368 entries is only `club, season, run` (how far the club got, e.g. `'Round of 16'`) plus a list of players and ratings. It never records who a club played or what the score was, so there's no real history of meetings to search.
- **There's no single "drafted club."** Your XI takes one player from each spin, so a squad mixes players from up to 11 different club-seasons. The run is labelled internally by pool and formation, not by a club.
- **Opponents are random and nothing is saved.** `sim()` and `createRun()` draw opponents at random from fixed lists (`OPP_POOL`, `TABLE_CLUBS`, etc.). The only thing kept between sessions is your best result (`fd_best`); individual matches are thrown away.

So "every time two clubs met across the dataset" has nothing to search yet.

**Ways this could work:**

1. **Add real historical head-to-heads (not recommended).** This would mean writing actual CL results (dates, scores, rounds) for every pairing across 1992–2026, thousands of matches. I'd be writing that from memory, so there's a real risk of wrong scorelines, and nothing in the repo could check them.
2. **Head-to-head from your own runs (recommended).** Save each completed run's matches in the browser. Credit a club when you draft one of its players (per match, weighted by or listed with those players), and count its games when it's the random opponent. Then a "Head to Head" screen lets you pick two clubs and see W/D/L, goals and a list of meetings. Everything shown comes from games you actually played in the app.
3. **Simulated head-to-heads from the dataset.** Pick two clubs, and the app plays their dataset squads against each other (every season pairing, or a chosen one) using the existing match engine, then shows the results. It makes nothing up, but the results are simulated, not history.

I'd go with option 2, and could add option 3's "simulate this matchup" button on the same screen if you'd like. For option 2 I also need you to pick how a drafted club counts: **(a)** it counts as having played if **any** of its players were in your XI, or **(b)** only the club with the most players in your XI counts.

Which option do you want?
