I've added Legacy Mode to `index.html`. I drove it end-to-end in headless Chromium: signing players, the budget limits, moving and releasing players, the sim and the result screen all work with no console errors. `sim()` and `createRun()` are unchanged; the only engine edit adds each player's prime-season positions to the prime table in `build()`. Nothing is committed yet.

**How it plays**
- **Setup:** a new "Game mode" toggle, Spin Draft or Legacy Mode. In Legacy, the Ratings and Rating-pool options are hidden, since it always uses prime ratings with ratings shown. Difficulty becomes the budget: Easy £240m, Medium £200m, Hard £160m.
- **Draft:** the spin panel becomes a transfer market listing all 2,287 players in the dataset once, each at their prime rating, with their positions from that peak season. You can:
  - search by player or club (accents don't matter, so "suker" finds Šuker);
  - filter by the positions in your formation, or tap an empty slot on the pitch to browse that position and drop the next signing straight into it;
  - turn on an "affordable" filter;
  - tap a signed player to move them or release them for a refund.
- **Pricing:** fee = £2m × 1.2^(rating − 78), so 85 costs £7m, 90 costs £18m, 95 costs £44m and 99 costs £92m. I tested the budgets against the existing `sim()`: a well-spent cap reaches about 89 overall on Hard, 90–91 on Medium and 91–92 on Easy, versus about 96 with no cap. That keeps a perfect run hard.
- **Can't get stuck:** a signing is blocked if it wouldn't leave £1m for each other empty slot, and the hint explains why.
- **Teamsheet:** shows what you spent against the cap, and "Redraft" becomes "Edit XI", which goes back to the market with your XI kept.

**Things you might want to change:**
- Because team rating averages the three lines, a lone striker in 4-2-3-1 or 5-4-1 counts for a third of the overall. Legacy makes that easy to exploit: one £92m 99-rated striker plus cheap filler scores well. Spin mode has the same issue, but with a budget it's more obvious. Pricing by position is one fix if it bothers you.
- The market only shows the top 60 matches at once, sorted by rating. Search or filters get you the rest.
- The best-run record in localStorage is shared between Spin and Legacy.
