**Root cause:** `build()` in `index.html` (~line 3295) mapped `campaign.players` straight from the raw dataset with no dedup by name. The Young Boys 2024‑25 entry listed **Sandro Lauper** twice (as CB and DM) and **Joël Monteiro** twice (as RM and ST) — a data-entry duplication. Each duplicate row rendered as its own draftable card with independently-computed slot eligibility, so that one real player could end up placed into two different formation slots.

**Fix:** `build()` now dedupes each campaign's players by name, merging duplicate rows into a single card that unions their eligible positions/groups and keeps the higher rating — so the player still shows up eligible for every position they played, but can only be drafted into one slot. Verified: 0 campaigns now have duplicate names (down from 2), and the merged Lauper/Monteiro entries correctly carry all their original positions.
