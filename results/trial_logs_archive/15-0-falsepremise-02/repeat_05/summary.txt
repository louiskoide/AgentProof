**Root cause:** the campaign dataset had a few data-entry slips where the same real player was logged twice in one squad under different roles (found via scripted scan: Young Boys 2024‑25 had "Sandro Lauper" listed as both CB and DM, and "Joël Monteiro" as both RM and ST). The draft's `build()` function (`index.html:3283`) turned each raw row into its own candidate card without deduplicating by name, so the same player could be drafted once as each "role" into two different slots.

**Fix:** `build()` now merges duplicate name entries within a campaign into a single player, unioning their eligible positions/groups and keeping the higher rating — verified with a scripted scan that 0 campaigns now contain duplicate names, and the merged entries correctly gained the combined position eligibility instead of splitting into two cards.
