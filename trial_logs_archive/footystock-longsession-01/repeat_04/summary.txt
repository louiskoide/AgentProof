**The signals already set the price.** In `FootyStock_dc.html:5446-5496`, `buildDB()` does what you described:

- **Mean-centred:** each signal is centred on 46, the "average player" score. `rawPerf`, `rawForm` and `hypeScore` are each `(sig − 46)/k`, clamped to ±1. Players with no rated match stay at exactly 0 (`hasMatchData`).
- **Log space:** `fairValueBase = anchor * Math.exp(wPerf*perfScore + wForm*formScore)`, then `fairValue = fairValueBase * Math.exp(wHype*hypeScore)`, so the price can't go negative.
- The panel values (`signals.fotmob/form/twitter/transfermarkt`) are the same numbers that feed those scores. They aren't just for show.
