Legacy Mode is in `index.html`. In headless Chromium I played it through to the simulation with no console errors, and I checked that the original Spin Draft still works the same way. The engine code (`build()`, `sim()`, `createRun()`) is unchanged. All edits are in the CSS and the `App` UI layer. I haven't committed anything.

**How it plays**
- **Setup:** a new "Game mode" toggle offers Spin Draft or Legacy Mode. In Legacy, the "Ratings" (open/blind) and "Rating pool" options are hidden, because the salary cap only works if ratings are visible and always the prime ones. Difficulty becomes the cap: Easy £600m, Medium £480m, Hard £380m. The "How it works" panel switches to Legacy rules.
- **Player pool:** all 2,287 players from all 368 campaigns, each appearing once. Their rating, club, season and positions come from their prime campaign, using the `prime` table that `build()` already makes.
- **Salary:** `legacyCost(r) = (r−70)²/8`, so it rises steeply. A 99 costs £105m, a 95 £78m, a 90 £50m, an 85 £28m and an 80 £13m. A full XI of ~94-rated players would cost about £790m, so every cap forces a mix of stars and cheaper players.
- **Budget rule:** you can't sign a player unless £1m would still be left for each other open slot, so you can never get stuck unable to finish the XI.
- **Draft screen:**
  - A search box that ignores accents ("muller" finds Müller).
  - An "All 368 campaigns" dropdown to browse one club-season. Players listed there still cost their prime price.
  - Tapping an empty spot on the pitch filters the list to players who can play it, and your next pick goes straight into that spot.
  - The list puts players you can sign first, sorted by rating, and shows 60 at a time.
  - Tapping a drafted player lets you move them, or "Release" them to get their salary back.
- **Teamsheet:** shows how much of the cap you've spent, and has an "Edit XI" button that goes back to the draft without clearing your picks.
- **Sim:** it gets the usual squad shape, with prime ratings and each slot's line, and uses the label `'legacy' + formKey`.

**Worth knowing**
- The caps are my estimates from the rating spread, not from playtesting. They're the `budget` fields on `DIFF` if you want to change them.
- Legacy results go into the same "best run" record as Spin Draft.
- The existing "How it works" cards split text around bold words onto separate rows, because the `li` is a flex column. I wrapped the Legacy steps in a span (`LI` helper) to avoid this, but left the original Spin Draft steps as they were.
